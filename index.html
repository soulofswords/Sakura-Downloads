Before you download

Start with the basics.

Windows

Windows 10/11 x64, WebGL-capable graphics and at least 8 GB RAM. 16 GB is preferred for local AI and streaming.

Android

Sakura Mobile requires Android 9 or newer with a current Android System WebView.

Your assets

Character models, RVC weights, local AI weights and provider keys are not bundled. Import or download your own.

Check the release notes and verify downloads with the included SHA-256 checksums. Install Companion and VTuber into separate folders.
